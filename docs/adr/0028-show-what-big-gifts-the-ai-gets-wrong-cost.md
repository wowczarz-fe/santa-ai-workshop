# 0028: Show what big gifts the AI gets wrong cost

Date: 2026-11-27

A player who checked the riskiest gifts, recalled one and ran out of money blamed the checks and
the recall. The real cost was big gifts the AI got wrong after half-time, and the game never said
so. Now each big gift the AI made or read wrongly shows its charge beside the budget when it is
caught for a remake ("The AI got a big gift wrong. Remaking it costs £50.") or reaches the sleigh
("... It counts £100 against the budget."), at most one line every 5 seconds with the ones in
between folded into the next, and a game the budget ends names its largest cost by cause, with
remakes and recalls weighed apart from wrong gifts aboard. Balance is unchanged.
