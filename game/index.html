ⓘ How these estimates work

Without checks assumes every wrong toy made would have shipped. It is not a simulated alternative policy. Prevented losses estimate the cost of caught mistakes. Estimated overchecking combines checking spend above estimated losses prevented with the estimated value of gifts left waiting for checks. Underchecking counts wrong gifts delivered without a check and their late recall or remake costs. Mistakes missed by a checker who looked are included in wrong gifts shipped, not in this underchecking cost.
