Santa's AI Workshop

About this page

Santa's AI Workshop is a short Christmas game in which the player runs Santa's workshop and decides how much to trust the AI and how much to check its work. Open the game in a new tab to see the texts where they appear.

Below is every text in the game, screen by screen. Please tell us wherever something is unclear, clumsy or wrong: suggest a better wording, leave a comment, or both. You do not need to touch every text. A text you leave alone counts as fine.

Reading everything takes about an hour. You can look only at the screens you care about, and you can stop and come back at any time. Words in braces, such as {child}, are filled in by the game, so please keep them in your suggestions.

When you are done, choose Send your feedback, copy your feedback and paste it into an email to the person who gave you this link.

Your feedback is saved in this browser as you type, so you can close the page and carry on later on the same computer.
