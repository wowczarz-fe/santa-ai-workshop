    How to keep Christmas alive
    Queues can end the gameAt capacity, a station jams for three seconds and a 16-second collapse countdown begins. Drain below 80% or buy storage. Throttle letter intake or stop the toy line to ease pressure upstream.
    Elves have limitsAt 88% fatigue, elves take a compulsory cocoa break. Idle elves also wander off. Recall refreshed elves. Below 45 morale, work slows; below 35 for 12 seconds, a strike begins. A £90 cocoa deal can settle it.
    Contain faulty batchesRoll back a bad update to protect future toys. Quarantine suspect parcels and recall gifts already loaded. Twelve suspect gifts aboard start an 18-second overload countdown. Tap parcels for individual decisions. Rewind failures for unranked practice.

    Top rating requires 155 correct gifts, fewer than 10% wrong gifts, morale of at least 45 and a balanced budget. Pausing is free. The outcome of checks is hidden until they happen.
